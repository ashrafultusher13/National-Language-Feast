01 ABOUT THE FEAST

More than just words.

THREE DAYS ∞ ENDLESS STORIES

Every language carries a world. Every story opens a door.

National Language Feast is a three-day celebration organized by Bangladesh Agricultural University Language Club, bringing together language, literature, creativity and imagination in one extraordinary experience.

From the beauty of literary expression to the fascinating worlds of anime and fiction, this feast invites participants to explore ideas, discover new perspectives and connect through the power of words.
